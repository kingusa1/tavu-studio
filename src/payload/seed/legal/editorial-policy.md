## 1. Who Writes Our Content

Every article published on the TAVÚ blog carries the name of the person who wrote it. Author profiles are linked from each article so you can see who is behind the words you are reading.

## 2. Health Information

Articles on this site provide general information about Reformer Pilates, Breathing Room classes, Contrast Therapy, Compression Therapy and massage. They are general information only — not medical advice, and not a substitute for a consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. Please consult a doctor before starting any new exercise, recovery or wellness practice, especially if you are pregnant, recovering from an injury, or managing a medical condition.

## 3. Accuracy and Sources

We aim to keep every article accurate and up to date. Where an article references a service, price, class or studio policy, it links to the relevant page on tavustudio.com so you can confirm the current details. Where we draw on an external source, we link to it directly.

## 4. Review and Updates

Each article shows the date it was first published and, when it has been revised, the date it was last updated. A named author — and where relevant, a named reviewer — stands behind each article.

## 5. Corrections

If you believe something in an article is inaccurate or out of date, please let us know using the contact details below. We will review the article and correct it where needed.

## 6. Independence

Our editorial content is independent. Any partnership, collaboration or sponsored content is clearly labelled as such.

## 7. Use of AI Assistance

Some articles may be drafted with the help of AI tools. Every article is reviewed, edited and approved by a named member of the TAVÚ team before publication, and that person is accountable for its accuracy. AI is never listed as the author of an article.

## 8. Images

Images used in our articles are photographs of TAVÚ Studio and its services, or licensed and stock images used to illustrate general concepts. Images are not intended to represent specific individuals or their results.
