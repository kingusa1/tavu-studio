Contrast Therapy at TAVÚ pairs hot and cold exposure to help your body recover and reset. Here's what a session includes.

## What's Included

TAVÚ's Contrast Therapy sessions combine the Himalayan Salt Sauna, Infrared Sauna and Ice Bath facilities. A session alternates between the saunas and the ice bath, which sits at 5–8°C.

Sessions run for 60 minutes and are available as:

- **Individual** — a private session
- **Couple** — for two, with a choice of a private or shared suite
- **Trio** — for three
- **Group** — for up to six guests

> Whether you're an athlete seeking faster recovery or simply looking to boost your wellness routine, Contrast Therapy is designed to leave you feeling refreshed and rejuvenated.

## The Benefits

- **Accelerated recovery** — reduces muscle soreness and speeds up recovery after intense workouts
- **Reduced inflammation** — cold therapy helps reduce inflammation and joint pain naturally
- **Improved circulation** — hot-cold contrast boosts blood flow and cardiovascular health
- **Mental clarity** — cold exposure releases endorphins and improves mental focus

## Add-Ons and Memberships

A Compression Flush can be added on to any Contrast Therapy session. For regular sessions, TAVÚ also offers the Reset and Ritual Unlimited recovery memberships.
