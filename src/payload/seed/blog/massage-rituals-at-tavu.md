## What Are TAVÚ's Massage Rituals?

TAVÚ's massage rituals are ladies-only therapeutic and relaxation massages, performed by certified massage therapists in private, serene treatment rooms, with every treatment tailored to whether you're after relaxation, pain relief or recovery.

## The Full-Body Rituals

Sixty or ninety minutes of focused, full-body work, each available as a Ritual Pack with a sixth session included:

- **FLOW | Relaxation Massage** — a slow, full-body ritual to soften muscular tension and calm the nervous system. AED 350 (60 min) / AED 500 (90 min); Ritual Pack AED 1,890 (5 + 1, valid 45 days).
- **RELEASE | Deep Tissue Massage** — deeper pressure to release muscular tightness and improve mobility. AED 350 (60 min) / AED 500 (90 min); Ritual Pack AED 1,890.
- **RECOVER | Sports Massage** — a therapeutic massage for active bodies, targeting muscular fatigue and restricted movement. AED 350 (60 min) / AED 500 (90 min); Ritual Pack AED 1,890.
- **DRAIN | Lymphatic Massage** — a gentle treatment to support lymphatic drainage and reduce fluid retention. AED 450 (60 min) / AED 500 (90 min); Ritual Pack AED 2,430.

## Targeted Recovery Rituals

Thirty-minute treatments offered individually as add-ons, or as a full 60-minute ritual with its own Ritual Pack (5 + 1, valid 45 days):

- **RESET | Head, Neck & Shoulders** — releases tension through the scalp, neck, shoulders and upper back. AED 300 (60 min ritual) / AED 150 (30-min add-on); Ritual Pack AED 1,620.
- **RECOVER | Deep Foot Recovery** — therapeutic massage, percussion therapy and targeted pressure-point work. AED 320 (60 min) / AED 150 (add-on); Ritual Pack AED 1,728.
- **RELEASE | Arm & Hand Recovery** — relieves tightness through the hands, wrists, forearms and arms. AED 300 (60 min) / AED 150 (add-on); Ritual Pack AED 1,620.
- **GROUND | Scalp Therapy** — a calming scalp ritual for deep relaxation. AED 300 (60 min) / AED 150 (add-on); Ritual Pack AED 1,620.
- **LIFT | Facial Recovery** — a sculpting facial massage supporting lymphatic drainage. AED 350 (60 min) / AED 150 (add-on); Ritual Pack AED 1,890.

## ROOTED and the Recovery Escape

**ROOTED | Foot Soaking & Reflexology** (AED 320, 60 min) pairs a 20-minute therapeutic foot soak — choose from the RECOVER, CALM or REVIVE signature salt blends — with a 30-minute reflexology foot massage and TAVÚ's signature herbal tea. Ritual Pack AED 1,728 (5 + 1, valid 45 days).

The **TAVÚ Recovery Escape** (AED 800, 3 hours) is the most complete ritual: exclusive access to the Main Contrast Suite — Himalayan salt sauna, ice bath and private showers — followed by a 60-minute full-body therapeutic massage and herbal tea. Appointments are limited.

## Choosing a Ritual

If you're not sure where to start, think in terms of what your body needs most: FLOW or RELEASE for general tension, RECOVER if you train regularly, DRAIN if you're dealing with fluid retention, one of the 30-minute targeted rituals for a specific area like the neck, feet, hands or scalp, ROOTED when you want a slower, grounding ritual, or the Recovery Escape when you want the full three-hour experience.

## What to Expect

Massage at TAVÚ is ladies-only, performed by certified therapists in private treatment rooms. Massage is widely regarded as a helpful way to manage everyday stress and muscle tension — Mayo Clinic notes that massage "can be a powerful tool to help you take charge of your health and well-being" and may help with stress, muscle tension and related discomfort (see Sources below), though it isn't a substitute for medical care. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have any concern, check with your doctor before booking.
