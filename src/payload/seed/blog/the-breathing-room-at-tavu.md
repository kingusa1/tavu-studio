## What Is the Breathing Room?

The Breathing Room at TAVÚ is a mat-based studio space dedicated to slowing down — home to the signature TAVÚ MAT, RELEASE and STRETCH classes, designed to restore balance, release tension and reconnect you to your own body.

Unlike the Reformer Room's machine-based work, every Breathing Room class happens on the mat, so the only equipment you need is yourself (and whatever props the instructor brings into class).

## Who It's For

With three distinct formats, the Breathing Room covers three different goals. TAVÚ MAT builds strength through classic mat Pilates work, scaling from a focused core class up to advanced sculpting. TAVÚ RELEASE is built for anyone carrying tension in the hips, lower back or whole body and wanting a gentler, mobility-focused class. TAVÚ STRETCH is for building flexibility and range of motion directly — useful on its own or as a complement to Reformer Pilates or other training.

## Three Signature Formats, 11 Classes

### TAVÚ MAT
Mat Pilates work, from a focused core class through to advanced sculpting:
- **TAVÚ MAT: Core** — strengthens the deep core, improves stability and supports better posture.
- **TAVÚ MAT: Power Pulse** (advanced) — a high-energy class of powerful movements, controlled pulses and challenging sequences.
- **TAVÚ MAT: Sculpt** (advanced) — challenging sculpting sequences that strengthen, define and test control and endurance.

### TAVÚ RELEASE
Classes built around easing tension and restoring mobility:
- **TAVÚ RELEASE: Hip Reset** — opens the hips and eases built-up tension through gentle movement and mobility work.
- **TAVÚ RELEASE: Lower Back Release** — releases tension through the lower back, hips and surrounding muscles.
- **TAVÚ RELEASE: Stretch & Release** — a calming full-body session to reduce tension and leave the body feeling lighter.

### TAVÚ STRETCH
Mobility and flexibility-focused classes:
- **TAVÚ STRETCH: Backbends & Posture** — opens the chest, shoulders, spine and hip flexors.
- **TAVÚ STRETCH: Balance + Sculpt** — balance work, controlled sculpting and stretching together.
- **TAVÚ STRETCH: Deep Core + Stretch** — deep core activation paired with full-body stretching.
- **TAVÚ STRETCH: Dynamic Stretching** — active, continuous movement to increase mobility and warm the body.
- **TAVÚ STRETCH: Split & Glutes** — targeted work for the hips, glutes and surrounding muscles.

## What to Expect

Classes run throughout the week — TAVÚ is open 9:00 AM–9:00 PM, 7 days a week. As with every class at TAVÚ, please arrive at least 10 minutes before your scheduled start time; a minimum of 4 participants is required for a class to proceed, and arriving 10 minutes or more after the start time means entry won't be permitted.

Stretching and mobility work is widely recommended as part of a balanced exercise routine. The NHS recommends a stretching routine after a workout "to gradually relax, improve flexibility and slow your heart rate" (see Sources below) — if you have an injury or a medical condition, check with your doctor before your first class.
