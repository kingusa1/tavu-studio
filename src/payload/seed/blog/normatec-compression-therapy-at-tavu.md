## What Is Compression Therapy at TAVÚ?

NormaTec Compression Therapy at TAVÚ is a recovery treatment that uses pulsing air compression to stimulate circulation and support faster muscle recovery — the same style of system used by professional athletes worldwide.

It's offered two ways at TAVÚ: as its own 45-minute Individual Session, or as a shorter add-on to a Contrast Therapy session when you want the extra recovery without adding much time to your visit.

## How the System Works

NormaTec's compression boots wrap around your legs and inflate and deflate in a sequential pattern, moving from your feet upward — a pulsing, wave-like pressure rather than a single constant squeeze. The pressure is adjustable, so a session can be set gentler for a first-timer or firmer for someone used to the system.

## Who It's For

Compression Therapy is most often booked by people training regularly who want a structured recovery habit, but it's just as suited to anyone on their feet all day or dealing with general tightness or swelling.

## What It's Used For

Compression Therapy at TAVÚ is positioned as a recovery aid rather than a treatment, recommended for:

- After intense training
- Heavy or tight legs
- Injury recovery
- Swelling or fluid retention

TAVÚ uses it as a recovery-focused treatment that stimulates circulation and supports muscle recovery. The research is still developing: a 2024 systematic review and meta-analysis of lower-limb intermittent pneumatic compression (the technology NormaTec uses) found trivial to moderate effects on perceived pain and soreness, trivial to small benefits for muscle function, and inconsistent results for lactate clearance (see Sources below) — so think of it as a comfort and recovery aid rather than a guaranteed performance fix.

Compression Therapy is a wellness and recovery practice, not a medical treatment. If you have a circulation condition, a blood clot history, or any other medical concern, check with your doctor before booking.
